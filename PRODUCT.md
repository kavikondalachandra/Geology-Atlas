# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Inferred from the request: students, educators, and curious visitors who want to identify and understand minerals, rocks, and soils in their geological settings.

## Product Purpose

An interactive, cross-platform atlas for exploring earth materials, their properties, formation, uses, and notable locations around the world.

## Positioning

The atlas connects specimen-level information to place and geologic time in one browsable experience.

## Operating Context

Inferred from the request: self-guided browsing on desktop, tablet, or mobile, with an option to open the experience locally or deploy it as a website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed requirements: detailed material profiles, color and classification, constituents and composition, uses, location details, high-resolution images, charts, graphs, a time scale, animation, smooth interaction, and a direct launcher. Implementation and data depth remain open. The standalone experience should not depend on a backend.

## Brand Commitments

The requested credit is “Concept & Design by K.S.Chandra 2026.”

## Evidence on Hand

No supplied image or research corpus. Educational facts should be attributed to reputable geological and scientific references; externally hosted image credits and links should be visible.

## Product Principles

- Connect each material to a real place and a formation process.
- Make scientific detail navigable without overwhelming first-time visitors.
- Attribute reference information and image sources clearly.
- Keep core browsing available without an account or backend.
